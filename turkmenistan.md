# STEP 1: TARGET IDENTIFICATION

Every effective effort begins by finding the exact harm and its source — and the external leverage, since domestic leverage barely exists. Vague concern goes nowhere, and domestic confrontation is impossible and unsafe; an effort aimed at "this super-emitting field or venting flare stack, measured by these satellites, run by the state gas company, tied to these buyers, which the methane diplomacy and the diaspora can press" is a real, workable effort — the kind Turkmenistan's external levers can deliver. Answer five questions.

## The Five Core Questions

**1. What is the harm, and what does it emit?** This is the first question here. What **methane, pollution, or environmental damage** does the source produce — the venting flare, the leaking field, the emissions — and how much.

**2. How is it seen from space?** Which **satellites and scientific bodies** have measured or could measure it, since the exposure is the foundation of every external lever.

**3. Which state companies and buyers are tied to it?** Which **state gas companies** run it and which **buyers and partners** are connected — the handles on the trade.

**4. Which external lever applies?** How the **methane diplomacy, the buyers, the economic-waste argument, and the diaspora** can press it — the reachable pressure.

**5. How is everyone inside kept safe?** Above all, how the effort proceeds **entirely from outside**, endangering no one inside — the absolute condition.

# STEP 2: DOCUMENTATION — HOW TO BUILD AN UNASSAILABLE CASE

Documentation is the foundation everything else stands on — and here, uniquely, most of it comes not from the ground, which is closed, but from satellites and science, which are open to all. A diplomat, a buyer, a scientist, and a journalist all move on evidence — and in Turkmenistan, a clear, satellite-proven record of the emissions and their source is precisely what an effort turns into a real result, so build it well, and from outside. Build your file in three layers.

## Layer 1: The Harm, the Source, and the Satellite Proof

Get the facts the case turns on — from space and science. Identify the **harm, its source, and its measured scale** — the super-emitting field, the venting flare, the leaking pipeline — and, decisively here, the **satellite and scientific evidence** that measures it, since this is the foundation no state secrecy can erase. The satellite datasets, the scientific studies, and the monitoring bodies that track methane are largely public and reachable. Establish exactly **what is emitted, where, and how it is measured**, because the satellite proof is the fight's unshakeable ground.

## Layer 2: The Scale — Climate, Waste, and Local Harm

Document the scale and the harm. Establish the **climate harm** — the quantity of methane, its outsized near-term warming, its place among the world's worst super-emitter events — and the **economic waste**, since the vented and leaked gas is lost revenue, drawing on **the satellite analyses, the scientific studies, and the international agencies' data**. And, where it can be done safely and from public sources, establish the **local and environmental harm** — the wider damage, the climate risk to the country's own people. Satellite imagery, scientific data, and public reporting make the harm undeniable, and the waste and climate stakes make it a global concern.

## Layer 3: The Harm, the Ties, the Lever, and the Safety

Finally, document the case and the plan. Does the **satellite and scientific evidence** prove the harm? How is it **tied to the state companies and the buyers**? Which **external lever** — the methane diplomacy, the buyers, the economic-waste argument, the diaspora — is open, and what does it require? And, always and above all, **how is the effort kept entirely external and everyone inside kept safe**? Each is a separate element, and together they make a case that can be pressed through the world that sees the harm, without endangering a soul inside. This is the layer that most often decides these efforts, because a satellite-proven harm, a tie to the trade, an open external lever, and absolute safety are exactly what let an effort reach a sealed state from outside. Build it well, and the satellite evidence, the methane diplomacy, and the gas buyers become the effort's genuine strength. One practical note on building this layer: the satellite evidence is not merely the most distinctive element of a Turkmen case but the element that makes a case possible at all, and it deserves the fullest attention. In almost every other country, the ground-level evidence — the testimony, the local documentation, the on-site investigation — is the heart of a case, and satellite data merely supplements it. In Turkmenistan, that ground-level evidence cannot be gathered safely, so the ordinary foundation of a case is simply unavailable; and it is precisely the satellite and scientific record that fills the void, supplying from orbit the proof that could never be gathered on the ground. So the file should be built, first and foremost, on the public satellite and scientific record — the datasets, the studies, the monitoring bodies' analyses — because that record is not only the strongest evidence available but very nearly the only evidence that can be gathered at all without endangering anyone.

And protect absolutely anyone inside — because in a state this closed and dangerous, an effort that presses a global harm through the world that can see it, while endangering no one inside, is the one that is both effective and just.

Turkmenistan sits on some of the world's greatest reserves of gas, and from its leaking fields and venting flare stacks pours one of the planet's largest streams of methane — a climate super-pollutant warming the sky the whole world shares — while inside its borders one of the most closed and repressive states on earth allows no press, no opposition, no voice, and denies even the truth of what its own satellites-visible fields emit; this guide has hidden none of it, nor pretended that a people so silenced can safely organise, or that a state so sealed is easily moved, and there is no landmark community victory here to promise you — not even the world's basic pledge to cut methane, which this state has spurned. But the harm is one the whole world can see from space, and that is the key: the satellites that cannot be blinded, the world's methane diplomacy, the buyers of the gas, the argument that the leaks are lost money, and the diaspora that carries the truth are all real levers that reach a sealed state from outside. There is no easy victory, and no landmark precedent to inherit; this guide has said so throughout, and insisted, above all, that no person inside be endangered. But the air of the whole world, and the voice of a silenced people, are worth defending, the external levers that reach even the most closed of states are real, and the patient, careful work of documenting the harm from space, pressing the world's methane diplomacy and the buyers, and carrying the truth through the diaspora is one that can genuinely be done — from outside, and safely, on behalf of a people who cannot yet do it themselves. So can you.
